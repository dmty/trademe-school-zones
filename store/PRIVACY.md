# Privacy Policy — School Zones for Trade Me

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

School Zones for Trade Me does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share personal data.

**What stays on your device.** The schools you select and a cached list of school names are saved in
your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device and are
removed when you uninstall the extension.

**Network requests.** To draw zones, the extension requests public school-zone data from an ArcGIS
Online service (services.arcgis.com, operated by Esri / Eagle Technology). These requests contain only
a school zone identifier, or — when you click "Zones at map centre" — the latitude/longitude of the
centre of the map you are viewing. No account, cookie, browsing history, or listing data is sent.
Esri's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

**Trade Me pages.** The extension runs on www.trademe.co.nz only to draw zone outlines on the property
map. It does not read, record, or transmit listing contents, your Trade Me account, or your activity.

**No analytics, ads, or tracking.**

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/dmty/trademe-school-zones/issues
